RESEARCH — ARE ROMAN NUMERALS USED?
================================================================
Yes — for the short form they are the standard.
  - The short (Mendeleev, 1871) form numbers its groups I..VIII in
    ROMAN numerals, printed above the columns. Each group splits
    into a главная (A) and a побочная (B) subgroup; the побочные
    are the d-elements.
  - The modern IUPAC scheme — Arabic 1..18 with no A/B — was
    adopted in the late 1980s (first printed ~1990) and applies to
    the LONG form. It exists precisely because the old Roman
    "IA / VIIB" labels are ambiguous: in the CAS scheme VIIB is
    manganese, in the old IUPAC scheme it is the halogens.
So: long form (index.html) -> Arabic 1..18;
    short form (short.html) -> Roman I..VIII + А/Б.
Periods are always Arabic (1..7).
Sources: ru.wikipedia "Короткая форма периодической системы
элементов", ru.wikipedia "Группа периодической системы",
en.wikipedia / wikidoc "Group (periodic table)".

Alignment: sources describe two printed variants — (a) главные
подгруппы aligned left, побочные right; (b) first row of a period
left, second row right. Variant (a) was used, since it makes the
subgroup readable at a glance.

WHAT WAS DONE
================================================================
New page short.html + short.js. No change to elements.json —
the short-form position is DERIVED from the long-form col/row:

  period   : row 1..7; lanthanide row 9 -> period 6,
             actinide row 10 -> period 7
  ряд      : periods 1-3 take one row; big periods take two —
             d-block half (col <= 10) first, p-block half
             (col >= 11) second  => 11 rows total
  group    : el.group (the Roman numeral added in task 3) is
             already the 8-group number
  subgroup : А (главная) for col 1-2 and 13-18 (s-/p-block),
             Б (побочная) for col 3-12 (d-block)

Layout (CSS grid in style.css):
  - column 1 = period number; for big periods the label spans both
    of its rows;
  - 8 group columns; header shows the Roman numeral with small
    А / Б labels underneath;
  - inside a group А is flush left and Б flush right, so K/Cu,
    Sc/Ga, Mn/Br sit on opposite sides;
  - group VIII is wider (3 cells) for the triads Fe Co Ni,
    Ru Rh Pd, Os Ir Pt, Hs Mt Ds;
  - La (57) and Ac (89) stay in group III Б as "La*" / "Ac**";
    58-71 and 90-103 are listed in two rows under the table.

Behaviour is the same as the long form: symbol -> atomic number on
hover, every cell is a link to /N.html. index.html and short.html
link to each other.

UPDATE (same day) — uniform column spacing
================================================================
First version put each group in ONE wide column and aligned А left
/ Б right inside it, so the distance between neighbouring symbols
jumped around (IА -> IБ was not the same as IБ -> IIА), and the
group VIII triads drifted.

Rebuilt as ONE uniform lattice instead:
  grid-template-columns: 40px repeat(17, 44px), gap 4px
  - groups I..VII  -> 2 columns each (А then Б)
  - group VIII     -> 3 columns (the triads); the noble gas sits
                      in the first of the three
  - column of an element = 2*group - 1 (А) or 2*group (Б);
    group VIII = 15 + (col - 8)
  - .slot wrappers dropped — every element is placed directly at
    its own grid-column/grid-row, like app.js does in the long form
  - header rows: row 1 = Roman numeral spanning its columns,
    row 2 = А / Б over the exact columns they describe
Now every column is 44px wide with the same 4px gap everywhere.

Re-verified: no two elements share a cell, all 17 columns are
used, А columns contain only главные подгруппы and Б columns only
побочные, headers span the right columns, and the 118/1..118
coverage still holds.
